## 🏆 What is VIP ERP?

> A full-featured, enterprise-grade accounting system built as a single-page application.  
> **Inspired by ERPNext V16. Designed for the Gulf. Runs without a backend.**

This is a production-quality frontend ERP system featuring:
- Full accounting suite (GL, AP, AR, COA, Journal Entries)
- Sales & purchase management with invoice generation  
- Real-time inventory tracking across multiple warehouses
- HR & payroll with GOSI integration (Saudi labor law)
- Financial reports (P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance)
- ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing compliance
- Bilingual AR/EN with full RTL support
